## What would flip the answer
1. **A same-country quote under E.** If a Colombia direct contractor or EOR comes in under ~$9,400-9,500/month all-in, F beats E on cost. It also shows how much of E's rate is vendor markup.
2. **E's terms don't hold up in writing.** If the engineer can be swapped without your approval, the rate excludes equipment or replacement, or real time-to-start runs well past 6 weeks, E's lead narrows. Its cost alone stays ahead: at 12 weeks to start it is still ~$11,300 per productive month.
3. **The board weights long-term ownership above cash.** If the board treats carrier integrations as knowledge that must sit with an equity-holding employee for 2+ years, A's ~2.2x cost per productive month may be a price they accept. That is a judgment call, not something the cost math settles. Also check overlap: Colombia is 1 hour ahead of Mountain in summer and 2 in winter, so the 4-hour overlap is not what separates A from E or F.

## Two options worth pricing for real
1. **E Staff augmentation:** it has the lowest cost per productive month of any quoted option and fits the 12+ month core-work horizon, 20 months of runway (30-day notice keeps it reversible) and the Mountain-time overlap. Get: the vendor's real time to a shortlist plus start, written inclusions (equipment, time off, replacement), and a named-engineer and no-swap clause.
2. **F-EOR or direct contractor in the same country:** this is the like-for-like test of E's premium, and it also suits long-horizon ownership. Get: an all-in monthly figure (salary + EOR fee + statutory employer costs) to compare with the $9,429 break-even, time to onboard, and termination costs.

The decision is yours; this memo only puts the options on the same scale.

## Questions to answer before you sign
- **All options:** Your CTO is heads-down and you have 5 hours a week. Who reviews this engineer's code and decides on integration architecture? If nobody, every option except D carries hidden risk.
- **A:** What does your benefits plan actually cost per employee, so 1.43x can be replaced? Will you use a recruiter, and at what percentage of $175k?
- **B:** Will he commit to 12 months in writing? Has counsel reviewed the full-time, open-ended arrangement for classification risk?
- **C:** What are the platform and payment fees, and does an all-in rate under $55.64/hr exist at the seniority EDI work needs?
- **D:** What does phase 2 cost? Who maintains the integration layer after month 4? Is the handover in the SOW?
- **E:** Is the engineer you interview named in the contract? What does $9,500 include? Is there a setup or placement fee? How long until the engineer actually starts?
- **F:** What is the EOR's all-in monthly cost and termination cost in Colombia? For a direct contractor, how will you verify identity and get the IP assignment?
